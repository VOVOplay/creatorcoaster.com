# Privacy Policy
**Last updated:** January 3, 2026

This privacy policy covers how data is handled when you use the Creator Coaster Discord server and website (`creatorcoaster.com`). Creator Coaster is an independent, community-driven project and is not affiliated with a registered company.

---

## Website
This website does not use cookies nor does it collect any information about you.

---

## Discord Server (bot)
We store this data for the operation of our Help Channels, Ticket System and other systems you choose to interact with. Specific ways we store your data include but are not limited to:
- Noting when you last successfully pinged with `@Request Help` for our cooldown feature.
- Storing your UserID when you create a ticket or open an application.
- Storing your UserID when you receive a punishment (e.g. warns, mutes, bans...).
- Storing the time of your last sent message so we can remove your "Helper Roles" if you go inactive.

When a ticket is closed, we create a transcript with all of the messages and attachments in that ticket. Our staff team has access to these transcripts for moderation purposes (such as punishment appeals). These transcripts are stored on discord directly in an encrypted format, in a channel where only current staff members can access them.

Data that is no longer needed for moderation or other purposes is usually deleted as soon as it becomes irrelevant for the functioning of the server.

---

### Data Removal:
Regardless of where you live, you can request a removal of your data under GDPR laws.
**Contact:** If you wish to remove your data, message `@vovoplay` or `@kdesa` on discord.
